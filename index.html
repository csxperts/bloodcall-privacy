Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Blood Call (“we”, “our”, “us”) is a blood donation assistance mobile application designed to connect blood donors and patients during emergency situations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

By using the Blood Call application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City
  • Blood group (optional)
  • Availability status (donor availability)

b. Account & Usage Data

  • Unique user ID (Firebase Authentication UID)
  • Blood requests created by users
  • Donation records (user-entered)
  • In-app chat messages

c. Device & Notification Data

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for notifications
We do NOT collect:
  • GPS or precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Financial or payment information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Display blood requests and donor availability
  • Enable in-app chat and calling between users
  • Send important notifications
  • Maintain optional donation history

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored securely using Google Firebase services
  • Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication
  • Access is restricted through Firebase security rules
  • Local sensitive data is protected using secure storage

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. Information is only visible to other users when required (e.g., name and city during blood requests).

5. Advertisements

Blood Call does not display advertisements. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated and users will be informed.

6. Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account directly from within the app.

  • User profile data is removed
  • Blood requests and donation records are deleted
  • Chat data associated with the account is removed
  • Authentication credentials are deleted

This action is irreversible.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Blood Call does not provide medical advice. The app only facilitates communication between donors and patients. Users are responsible for consulting healthcare professionals.

8. Children’s Privacy

Blood Call is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page and inside the app.
